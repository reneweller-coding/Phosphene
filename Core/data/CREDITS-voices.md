# Voices -- sources and credits

`Core/data/voices.phosvx` holds 31 spoken phrases, 59.6 s in all, 478740 bytes (16 kHz mono, 4-bit IMA ADPCM),
built by `Tools/voice_pack.py` from `Tools/voice_selection.json` (round "fx-psychedelia", 19.09.2026).
Every phrase was cut from a file of the AmbientSynth archive (`AmbientSynth/Library/Archive`, whose
`SOURCES.md` records where each file came from); the rights below are the sources' own statements.

## What was left out, and why

- **Radio/Quiet-Please** (120 phrases of the 1947-49 radio series): the archive's case for them rests on
  the EU terms (the writer died in 1955, the recordings' neighbouring rights expired) and on the uploader's
  public-domain tag. That is enough for a private instrument, not for a pack that ships inside a plugin and
  an APK anywhere; the US status of the broadcasts is not established. Not used.
- **LoC/Joe-Smith** (250 interview clips): reusable with attribution, but the speakers are identifiable
  figures of the music business, many alive; their voices inside generated tracks could read as an
  endorsement. Not used.
- **LoC/Variety-Stage** and **LoC/Edison**: public domain, but comic sketches and songs whose lines do
  not work as spoken word over a psytrance groove. The one Edison line tried ("the energy of that
  machine") failed the check below: the cylinder is too noisy for word timestamps.

**The check.** Every packed phrase was decoded again by an independent decoder and transcribed once
more (faster-whisper, base model): the words are the ones listed above.
- **NASA/Beyond, NASA/Historical/Beeps, Missions, NASA/Sonification**: recordings and sonifications, not
  speech -- material for a texture round, not for a voice pack. The LCROSS song is third-party music.
- **The EchoThief impulse responses and everything under AmbientSynth's own sample folders** are outside
  this archive and were not considered.
